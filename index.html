Night Summary

The Install

Fit 768 lights across the 12 Gandel Hall columns before the morning clean-up.

Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, E to act, F to drop, Space and Shift move the lift deck.

Phone: left stick moves, right stick looks (hold it over to keep turning), tap the message on the right to do what it says.

On the lift, walk to the control box and ACTION to drive: forward and back drives, left and right steers the front wheels. ACTION again lets go so you can walk the deck and handle lights.

What is in the model

Columns12, gloss black steel, tapering shaft with 8 fins (10 mm wide, 50 mm proud at the foot to 150 mm at the ceiling)
StripsENTTEC 8PXA60-RGBW-12V-B pixel strip (60 RGBW LED/m, 4,000 K white die, black PCB, 10 mm) in a 20 × 20 mm aluminium extrusion with a black acrylic diffuser (options: opal cover passing ~65%, or open channel), one per fin gap, floor to glass
LED stripsUp to 8 per column, one per fin gap, on the shaft facing outward. Every strip is 60 LEDs/m; the pixel density option (60 px/m, 12 V 20 px/m, 24 V 10 px/m) sets how many LEDs share a colour. Count, density, colours and animation are set in the panel above and drawn live
